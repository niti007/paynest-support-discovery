# Master Services Agreement: Schedule 3, Support Service Levels (excerpt)

*Template v3.2, effective for contracts signed or renewed on or after 1 January 2025. Extract prepared by Legal for the support team; if this extract and the signed contract differ, the signed contract wins.*

## 3.1 Support tiers

| Tier | Who gets it | Support hours | First response target | Resolution target | SLA credits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Enterprise** | Named-account customers (annual value > USD 30,000 or by negotiation) | 24x5 + payroll-week weekend cover | P1: 1 h, P2: 4 h, P3: 8 h | P1: 8 h, P2: 24 h, P3: 72 h | Yes |
| **Business** | Mid-market, named CSM | 08:00-20:00 local, Mon-Sat | P1: 2 h, P2: 8 h, P3: 24 h | P1: 12 h, P2: 48 h, P3: 96 h | Yes |
| **Standard** | Everyone else | 09:00-18:00 local, Mon-Fri | Best effort | Best effort | No |

*Hours in the table are elapsed (calendar) hours measured from ticket creation, not business hours.*

## 3.2 Priority definitions

- **P1**: payroll cannot be run, or employees will not be paid on time, or statutory filing will be missed.
- **P2**: material error in payroll output, or a core module is degraded with a workaround.
- **P3**: how-to, reports, cosmetic, configuration, and everything else.

Priority is set by the customer when raising a ticket and may be adjusted by Support with notice.

## 3.3 Measuring resolution time

Resolution time runs from ticket creation to the **final** resolved event. If the customer reopens a ticket within 7 days, the clock is not paused or restarted: the reopened time counts towards the original resolution time.

## 3.4 Service credits

If the resolution target for a ticket is missed, the customer is entitled to a credit against next month's subscription, calculated as a percentage of the **monthly subscription fee**:

| Tier | P1 | P2 | P3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | 10% | 5% | 2% |
| Business | 5% | 3% | 1% |

**Severity multiplier:** if the actual resolution time exceeds **twice** the target, the credit percentage is multiplied by **1.5**.

Credits are per breached ticket. Under template v3.2 there is **no monthly cap** (earlier templates capped at 25%). Credits are applied automatically by Finance from the breach report and approved by the Head of Support.

## 3.5 Exclusions

Time awaiting information from the customer is excluded **only** where the ticket has the status "Pending - customer" for that interval. Planned maintenance and force majeure are excluded.
