# Ticket Triage SOP

**Owner:** Support Operations  |  **Version:** 4.1  |  **Last reviewed:** 14 Feb 2025

## Purpose
Make sure every ticket reaches the correct team on first touch and gets a first response inside the SLA.

## 1. Intake
1. Tickets arrive from email, the in-app widget, the partner/reseller portal, WhatsApp (shared inbox), and phone (agent logs a ticket after the call).
2. **Every ticket is categorised at intake.** The customer or the agent selects one of the nine categories from the dropdown. The category cannot be left blank.
3. Category drives **automatic routing** to the owning queue. No manual routing is required.

| Category | Queue |
|---|---|
| Payroll | Payroll-Ops |
| Statutory | Statutory-Compliance |
| How-To | General-L1 |
| Reports | Reports-Analytics |
| Leave & Attendance | Leave-Attendance |
| Integration | Integrations |
| Billing | Billing |
| Access / Login | Access-Support |
| Onboarding | Onboarding |

## 2. Handling
4. L1 agents resolve at first touch **using the Confluence Knowledge Base**. The KB is the single source of truth; agents must not answer from memory.
5. Reply with the relevant KB article ID so the case history shows the source.
6. A ticket may be **reassigned at most once**. If it needs to move a second time, the Support Lead must approve and re-categorise it.
7. All KB articles are **reviewed at least every 90 days** by their owner. Articles not reviewed in 90 days are flagged in the monthly KB report.

## 3. Closure
8. Resolve the ticket only when the customer has confirmed, or after 48 hours without response.
9. Reopened tickets keep their original priority and go back to the original assignee.

## 4. Quality
10. Target first-response compliance: 95%. Target first-touch resolution: 70%.
11. Any suspected personal data pasted into a ticket should be masked by the agent before the ticket is shared internally.
