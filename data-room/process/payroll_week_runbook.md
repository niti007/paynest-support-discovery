# Payroll Week Runbook

**Owner:** Support Operations  |  **Version:** 3.0  |  **Last reviewed:** 9 Jun 2025

"Payroll week" is the 25th of the month to the 3rd of the next month, when most customers run payroll, file statutory returns and release salaries.

## T-10 days (by the 15th)
1. Compliance India and Compliance UAE send Support a **statutory change notice** for anything taking effect in the coming cycle.
2. Content Team **updates the affected KB articles** before the change goes live and updates the "last reviewed" date.
3. Support Operations briefs all L1/L2 agents in the weekly huddle.

## T-3 days
4. Shift roster published. **All leave is blocked** for L1 and L2 from the 25th to the 3rd.
5. Payroll-Ops queue is staffed with **at least 40% of L1 agents plus 3 L2 specialists** at all times.
6. Auto-reply enabled with the link to "Payroll week FAQs".

## During payroll week
7. First response targets are halved for P1 payroll tickets (30 minutes).
8. Agents must not re-categorise tickets without Lead approval.
9. If the same issue is reported by 5+ employees of one customer, the Lead declares an incident.
10. Daily 17:00 stand-up: top issues, backlog age, SLA risk list.

## T+3 days
11. Retrospective: tickets breaching SLA are reviewed by the Support Lead.
12. Credits are processed by Finance from the breach report.
