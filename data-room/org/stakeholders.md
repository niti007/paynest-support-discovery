## Known tensions
- CEO and VP Sales want different "AI things" (text chatbot vs voice bot) and neither has looked at ticket data.
- CFO and Head of Support disagree on whether headcount reduction is the goal or the outcome.
- Engineering and Support disagree on who owns knowledge about statutory changes.
- Everyone agrees "payroll week is a nightmare"; nobody agrees why.
